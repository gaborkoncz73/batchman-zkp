# Figure style

All figures use a white ground, Arial/Helvetica-compatible sans serif text, a 10 px/mm SVG view box, and 0.6–1.0 pt final line weights. Body labels are designed for 7.5–8.5 pt at print size. Color and line treatment are semantic and invariant across the set.

| Meaning | Color |
|---|---|
| Public/trusted data | `#245A8D` dark blue |
| Private/witness data | `#168A83` teal |
| Policy/compiler/static structure | `#536575` slate |
| Cryptographic binding | `#7256A8` violet |
| Valid/success | `#2C8B57` green |
| Warning/inconclusive | `#D58A16` amber |
| Rejected/adversarial | `#C84A3A` vermilion |
| Neutral structure | `#E9EDF0` light gray |
| Text/borders | `#263238` charcoal |

Solid lines denote implemented/enforced relations. Dashed lines denote metadata, derivation, or offline flows. Dotted lines denote optional or proposed elements. Vermilion dashed lines denote rejected attacks. Shape, text labels, and line patterns duplicate every color-coded distinction for grayscale use.

Double-column figures are 180 mm wide. Figure 5 is 89 mm wide. SVG text remains selectable; PDF exports are vector; PNG exports are 600 dpi.
